# Spec 01 — Preserve Route Topology and Access Boundaries

**Priority:** P1  
**Depends on:** Spec 00

## Issue intent

Maintain and extend the current route topology without reintroducing obsolete route or access assumptions.

## Current topology

```text
app/
├── page.tsx
├── (public)/
│   ├── faq/
│   ├── privacy/
│   └── terms/
├── (auth)/
│   ├── sign-in/
│   └── sign-up/
├── (setup)/
│   └── onboarding/
├── (tenant)/
│   ├── admin/
│   ├── ai/
│   ├── crm/
│   ├── dashboard/
│   ├── expenses/
│   ├── invoices/
│   ├── marketing/
│   ├── my-tasks/
│   ├── portal/
│   ├── projects/
│   ├── settings/
│   ├── social/
│   └── support/
└── api/
```

`app/page.tsx` owns `/` directly.

Do not move it into `(public)` solely for conceptual uniformity.

## Public routes

Public informational routes use the public shell/layout where implemented.

Do not create `/features`, `/pricing`, `/contact`, or any other route merely because an older spec listed it.

The landing page may use anchors or in-page sections where that is the current product behavior.

## Tenant routes

`app/(tenant)/layout.tsx` owns the top-level tenant gate.

It must continue to:

- require application identity;
- redirect unauthenticated users to sign-in;
- require completed onboarding;
- redirect incomplete setup to `/onboarding`.

Do not restore anonymous tenant browsing without explicit owner instruction.

## URL semantics

Route groups do not create URL segments.

Real URL segments such as `admin` remain real directories under the tenant group.

The invoicing business area currently uses `/invoices` and `/expenses`.

Do not create `/invoicing` for naming symmetry.

## Page responsibility

Route pages should remain thin when the surface already has a feature or template.

They may own:

- params/search-param adaptation;
- Suspense;
- route metadata;
- loading/error/not-found behavior;
- redirect outcomes;
- selection of the feature/template entrypoint.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `/` remains served by the implemented root page unless the owner requests otherwise.
- [ ] Public informational routes remain accessible without tenant admission.
- [ ] `/sign-in` and `/sign-up` remain auth entrypoints.
- [ ] `/onboarding` remains the setup route.
- [ ] Tenant routes require authentication and completed onboarding.
- [ ] Admin remains under a real `/admin` segment.
- [ ] Invoicing URLs remain `/invoices` and `/expenses` unless explicitly changed.
- [ ] No stale anonymous-demo requirement is reintroduced.
- [ ] Route pages remain thin where existing feature/template boundaries support that pattern.
