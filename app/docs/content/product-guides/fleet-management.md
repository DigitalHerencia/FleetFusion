# Fleet management

The fleet area maintains the organization’s trucks, trailers, and service vehicles. Accurate asset records support dispatch readiness, maintenance planning, inspections, and vehicle document tracking.

## Add and maintain a vehicle

1. Gather the identifying and operational details required by your organization.
2. Create the vehicle record using the appropriate asset type.
3. Review the saved details for accuracy before the vehicle is used in an assignment.
4. Update the record when ownership, availability, or other tracked information changes.

FleetFusion supports vehicle creation, updates, and removal, as well as bulk import. Validate imported rows before upload and review the resulting records afterwards.

## Track upkeep and inspections

Use maintenance records and inspection history to keep equipment information current. Enter dates and outcomes promptly, and ensure a maintenance or inspection record is associated with the right vehicle. Follow your company’s maintenance and safety procedures; software records do not replace required inspections.

## Vehicle documents

Vehicle compliance documents are managed as part of the compliance workflow. Link a document to the correct vehicle and keep its expiration information current. See [Compliance workflow](/docs/user-docs/compliance) for review and follow-up guidance.

## Before assigning a vehicle

- Confirm that the vehicle is the intended unit and its record is current.
- Review any known maintenance, inspection, or compliance concerns.
- Confirm operational availability with your team.
- Coordinate the vehicle with an available driver through [dispatching](/docs/product-guides/dispatching).
