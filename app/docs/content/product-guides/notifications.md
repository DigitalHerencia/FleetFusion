# Notifications

FleetFusion routes operational alerts to supported channels, including in-app notifications, email, and SMS. Delivery depends on notification type, user preferences, and configured channel availability.

## Review in-app notifications

Check the notification list and unread count for updates that need attention. Open the related operational record to verify current details before acting. Mark a notification read when you have reviewed it; use the mark-all-read action only after checking that you will not lose track of pending work.

## Set personal preferences

Use notification preferences to choose supported delivery channels for the types of updates you receive. Preferences affect delivery routing, not the underlying organization record or required operational follow-up. Keep an appropriate channel enabled for time-sensitive work and make sure contact details are current through your organization’s approved process.

## Troubleshoot missing notifications

1. Check the in-app notification list and unread count.
2. Confirm your preference for the notification category and channel.
3. Verify that the event occurred and that you have access to the related record.
4. Check whether the message was filtered by the destination email or phone provider.
5. Ask an administrator to review notification configuration if the issue continues.

Not every event is delivered to every channel. Do not rely on notification delivery as the sole record of a load status, compliance deadline, or other business event.
