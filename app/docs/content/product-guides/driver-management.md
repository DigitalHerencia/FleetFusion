# Driver management

Maintain driver profiles and qualifications in one place so dispatchers and compliance staff can make informed decisions. The driver record supports a broader workflow that includes history, shift activity, and documents.

## Create or update a driver profile

1. Collect the driver’s organization-approved profile and contact details.
2. Create the driver record and verify the saved details.
3. Keep qualification information such as CDL and medical-document details current.
4. Use the driver history when reviewing prior activity or changes.

Driver records can be updated, removed, or bulk-imported by authorized users. For imports, validate the input and reconcile the resulting records before using them operationally.

## Shifts and mobile workflows

The driver workflow supports starting and ending a shift, syncing location, and uploading driver documents. Drivers should follow their organization’s process for beginning and ending work and report any location or document-sync issue to their dispatcher or administrator.

FleetFusion’s shift records support operational visibility; they are not a substitute for applicable hours-of-service requirements, ELD records, or regulatory obligations.

## Driver documents and compliance

Use the compliance workflow to keep required driver documents and expiration dates visible. Review upcoming alerts and update records after renewal. See [Compliance workflow](/docs/user-docs/compliance).

## Assignment readiness

Before assigning a driver to a load, confirm the driver is available and has the qualifications required by your organization. Coordinate the driver with an appropriate vehicle in [dispatching](/docs/product-guides/dispatching), and record status changes as work progresses.
