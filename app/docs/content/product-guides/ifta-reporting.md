# IFTA reporting

The IFTA workflow brings trip mileage and fuel-purchase records together to support quarterly fuel-tax reporting. Complete source records throughout the quarter so the summary can be reviewed before it is used.

## Maintain source records

- **Trips:** Add trip details and mileage using the information captured by your organization. Correct entries when verified source information changes.
- **Fuel purchases:** Record each purchase with its relevant date, jurisdiction, quantity, and supporting details as required by your organization’s process.
- **Review:** Check for missing dates, unexplained gaps, duplicates, and mismatched trip or fuel details before generating a report.

FleetFusion supports creating, updating, and deleting trip and fuel purchase records. Changes should be based on verified source documents.

## Prepare a quarterly report

1. Select the intended reporting quarter and verify the organization context.
2. Review the trip and fuel records included for that period.
3. Check the available jurisdiction rate information and summary totals.
4. Generate the quarterly report and review the result for missing or implausible values.
5. Resolve source-record issues and regenerate or update the report as needed.

The system can generate a quarterly report and retrieve summaries and rate tables. Validate the report against source records and applicable filing guidance before submitting it to a tax authority; FleetFusion does not replace professional tax advice.

## If totals look wrong

Check the reporting period first, then inspect the underlying trips, mileage, fuel transactions, and jurisdiction data. Missing or inaccurate source records can change the summary. If you cannot edit a record, confirm your IFTA permissions with an administrator.
