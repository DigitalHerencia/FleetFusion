# Dispatching

The dispatch workflow manages loads from creation through assignment and status tracking. The dispatch board gives authorized users a place to review work and coordinate drivers and vehicles.

## Create a load

Enter the load information required by your organization and verify important dates and locations before saving. Correct load details make assignment and later status review more reliable.

## Assign a driver and vehicle

1. Check the load details and operational requirements.
2. Confirm a driver is available and has the required qualifications.
3. Confirm a suitable vehicle is available and its record is current.
4. Assign the driver to the load and verify the assignment on the board.

Availability is resolved by dispatch rules and current assignments. Do not rely only on an old status or a notification; confirm the current record before assigning.

## Track load status

Update the load status as the work moves through its lifecycle. FleetFusion applies transition rules to help keep status changes consistent and records status events for history. If the requested transition is unavailable, verify the current status and follow your organization’s escalation process instead of skipping lifecycle steps.

## Maintain the board

- Review unassigned or recently changed loads at shift handoff.
- Follow up on loads whose status has not advanced as expected.
- Correct load or assignment details promptly if they change.
- Use the [daily operations checklist](/docs/user-docs/daily-operations) for a repeatable shift routine.

## Common issues

| Issue                                  | Check                                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Driver is not available for assignment | Review current driver assignments and shift availability |
| Vehicle is not suitable or visible     | Confirm the vehicle record and organization context      |
| Status update is rejected              | Verify the current status and allowed next transition    |
| A load is not visible                  | Check filters, organization access, and your role        |
