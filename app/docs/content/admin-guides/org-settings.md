# Organization settings

Organization settings define shared workspace information and provide administrators with a view of membership and subscription details. Only users with the required organization permissions can make changes.

## Maintain the organization profile

Review the organization profile for accurate name and contact information. Use the profile update workflow to apply verified changes, then confirm the updated details are visible to the organization. Keep personal information to the minimum needed for the operating purpose.

## Manage membership

Review the organization member list regularly. Invite users through the approved invitation process and assign roles based on their responsibilities. Remove or adjust access when a person changes roles or leaves the organization. See [Users and roles](/docs/admin-guides/users-and-roles).

## Review subscription information

Check the subscription details and plan limits when planning a change in fleet size or new workflow. Available capabilities can depend on plan and organization feature configuration. See [Subscriptions and billing](/docs/admin-guides/billing).

## Safe administration

- Use your own administrator account and avoid sharing credentials.
- Confirm the active organization before changing any settings.
- Review the impact on organization members before changing access or enabled features.
- Ask a super-admin for help with platform-level system administration; organization settings do not grant super-admin access.

For product access and security detail, see [Feature availability](/docs/admin-guides/feature-flags) and the [security reference](/docs/reference/security).
