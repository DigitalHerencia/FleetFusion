# Subscriptions and billing

Subscription information describes the organization’s plan and the capabilities or usage limits associated with it. Review the current subscription before changing team size or enabling a workflow.

## Documented plan comparison

The product requirements describe the following tiers. Treat these figures as a plan reference; confirm current pricing, contract terms, and availability with your account administrator or billing provider before making a purchase decision.

| Tier       | Published price in product requirements | Fleet and user limits                                       | Notable capabilities                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Starter    | $149/month                              | Up to 5 trucks, 2 dispatchers, 5 driver-app users           | Dispatch, vehicle and driver management, 5 GB document storage, basic compliance; IFTA and custom reports are not listed |
| Growth     | $349/month                              | Up to 25 trucks, unlimited dispatchers, 25 driver-app users | Dispatch, vehicle and driver management, advanced compliance, IFTA reporting, custom reports                             |
| Enterprise | $15/truck/month, $699 minimum           | Unlimited                                                   | Advanced compliance with audit capability, IFTA reporting, and custom reports                                            |

Plan offerings and billing can change. This table reflects the product requirements, not a live quote or invoice.

## Review plan fit

1. Compare the planned fleet and user count with the plan limits.
2. Confirm which capabilities the organization needs, including document storage, IFTA, and custom reporting.
3. Review the subscription information in organization settings.
4. Contact the account owner or billing administrator for plan changes, invoicing, or contract questions.

## Access and feature differences

An action may be unavailable because of the user’s role, the organization’s subscription, or an organization-level feature setting. Check all three before assuming a system error. See [Feature availability](/docs/admin-guides/feature-flags) and [Users and roles](/docs/admin-guides/users-and-roles).
