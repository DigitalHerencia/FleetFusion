# FleetFusion documentation

FleetFusion brings dispatch, fleet and driver records, compliance tracking, IFTA preparation, and operational reporting together for trucking organizations. Use these guides to learn a workflow, then follow the link to the relevant topic for more detail.

## Choose a starting point

- **New to FleetFusion?** Start with [Getting started](/docs/user-docs/getting-started), then complete [organization onboarding](/docs/user-docs/onboarding).
- **Running a shift?** Use [Daily operations](/docs/user-docs/daily-operations) to coordinate loads, drivers, and equipment.
- **Managing a specific area?** Browse [Product guides](/docs/product-guides) for fleet, driver, dispatch, IFTA, and notification workflows.
- **Administering a workspace?** See [Admin guides](/docs/admin-guides) for organization settings, roles, subscriptions, and feature availability.
- **Looking for technical policy?** Visit [Reference](/docs/reference) for configuration, security, and observability notes.

## Access and availability

FleetFusion is multi-tenant: your organization’s records are scoped to its workspace. Available actions are controlled by your assigned role, and some capabilities depend on the organization’s subscription. If a guide describes a capability you cannot access, contact your organization administrator rather than trying to work around the restriction.

Continue to [the product overview](/docs/overview) for the platform’s operating model, or browse a section from the navigation.
