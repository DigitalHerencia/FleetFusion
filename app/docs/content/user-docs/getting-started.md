# Getting started

Welcome to FleetFusion. Use this guide to orient yourself, confirm your organization context, and find the workflows you will use most often.

## 1. Sign in and confirm your workspace

Sign in through the organization’s configured account. FleetFusion uses your authenticated account and organization membership to determine which workspace and actions are available. If you belong to more than one organization, verify the active context before changing records.

If you cannot sign in or do not see an expected workspace, contact your organization administrator. Do not share your password or invitation link with another person.

## 2. Find your day-to-day area

- Dispatchers: begin with [Dispatching](/docs/product-guides/dispatching) and review the [daily operations checklist](/docs/user-docs/daily-operations).
- Fleet managers: review [fleet management](/docs/product-guides/fleet-management) and [driver management](/docs/product-guides/driver-management).
- Compliance staff: start with the [compliance workflow](/docs/user-docs/compliance).
- Accountants: review [IFTA reporting](/docs/product-guides/ifta-reporting) and [analytics](/docs/user-docs/analytics).
- Administrators: complete [organization onboarding](/docs/user-docs/onboarding) and [users and roles](/docs/admin-guides/users-and-roles).

## 3. Learn the key records

FleetFusion links operational work to organization data:

| Record                      | Purpose                                                                       |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vehicle                     | A truck, trailer, or service vehicle in the fleet inventory                   |
| Driver                      | A person who can be managed, assigned, and tracked through shift workflows    |
| Load                        | A dispatch job whose assignment and status are followed through its lifecycle |
| Compliance document         | Evidence associated with a driver or vehicle, with expiration follow-up       |
| IFTA trip and fuel purchase | Mileage and fuel records used to prepare quarterly summaries                  |

Keep records accurate and current. Correct vehicle, driver, document, mileage, and fuel details make downstream dispatch and reporting more useful.

## 4. Know how access works

Actions are governed by organization roles and server-side permissions; seeing a link or page does not grant permission to change every record. Some features can also depend on the subscription plan. Ask an administrator to review access if a workflow is unavailable.

## Next steps

Complete [onboarding](/docs/user-docs/onboarding) if your workspace is new. For an established organization, go to [daily operations](/docs/user-docs/daily-operations) or browse all [product guides](/docs/product-guides).
