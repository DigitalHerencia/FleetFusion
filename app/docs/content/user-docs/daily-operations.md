# Daily operations

Use this sequence as a practical start-of-shift and end-of-shift routine. It links the dispatch board to the fleet, driver, document, and notification workflows it relies on.

## Start of shift

1. **Review alerts.** Check unread notifications and prioritize assignment changes or compliance items that affect today’s work.
2. **Check resource readiness.** Confirm the assigned vehicle and driver records are current. Review relevant vehicle inspection and maintenance information and driver qualifications.
3. **Review the dispatch board.** Check loads that need creation, assignment, or follow-up. Use the [dispatching guide](/docs/product-guides/dispatching) for the load lifecycle.
4. **Assign suitable resources.** Confirm that the selected driver and vehicle are available and appropriate before assigning them to a load.

## During the shift

- Record load status as work progresses; status updates help teammates understand where a load stands.
- Keep trip and fuel records current if your organization uses IFTA workflows. See [IFTA reporting](/docs/product-guides/ifta-reporting).
- Report missing or inaccurate vehicle, driver, or document information to the person responsible for maintaining that record.
- Use notifications to follow up on relevant updates, but treat the record in FleetFusion as the source to verify before taking operational action.

## End of shift

1. Confirm load statuses and assignment details are up to date.
2. Complete applicable driver shift and document workflows.
3. Check for open compliance items or records that need correction.
4. Review operational summaries and leave a clear handoff for the next shift.

## Weekly review

Review upcoming document expirations, planned vehicle maintenance, incomplete driver or trip records, and operational KPIs. Use the [compliance workflow](/docs/user-docs/compliance) and [analytics guide](/docs/user-docs/analytics) for those reviews.

## When a task is unavailable

Your role and organization plan determine which actions are available. Contact an administrator if you need access; do not use another person’s account.
